# Sporting2Impact logo files

Master vector (SVG) versions of the logo. These are **not** served on the website
(the site uses `public/Sporting2Impact.png`); they are kept here for future edits and
for sharing with partners.

## Files

| File | Use on |
|---|---|
| `Sporting2Impact.svg` | White or light backgrounds (transparent background) |
| `Sporting2Impact-light.svg` | Dark or colored backgrounds (navy parts are white) |
| `Sporting2Impact-badge.svg` | Any background (logo on a white rounded square), best choice for partners |
| `Sporting2Impact-badge-circle.svg` | Any background (logo on a white circle) |

### Black and white

| File | Use for |
|---|---|
| `Sporting2Impact-black.svg` | One-color black: B&W flyers, newspaper ads, stamps, single-color T-shirts on light fabric |
| `Sporting2Impact-white.svg` | One-color white: single-color printing on dark fabric or dark backgrounds |
| `Sporting2Impact-grayscale.svg` | Shades of gray: when a gray version is specifically requested |
| `Sporting2Impact-badge-grayscale.svg` | Grayscale badge for any background |

For black-and-white printing, prefer the one-color black version: in grayscale the
orange sun and cyclist turn light gray and can look washed out on copiers.

SVGs scale to any size without blurring. To get a PNG, open the SVG in a browser,
Inkscape, Figma or Canva and export at the size you need (2000 px is good for print
and social media). Please don't stretch, recolor or crop the logo.

## Fonts

The lettering is drawn as outlines (no font needed to open the files). It was
matched to the original logo using free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License):

- "SPORTING", "I A C T" and the "2": **Poppins Bold**
- "P": Poppins ExtraBold; "M": Montserrat ExtraBold

For matching flyers or social posts, use **Poppins Bold**.

## How these were made

Rebuilt in Nov 2026 from the original 500 px PNG: the artwork was traced to
vectors, the text was replaced with matching font outlines, and the ball, the arc
where the arm crosses it, and the ball player's arm and hand were redrawn as clean
shapes. For large-format print (banners, T-shirts), a designer's review is still
recommended.
