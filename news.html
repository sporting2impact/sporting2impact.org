New ProgramNovember 8, 2026

Coming soon: Mixed Fitness, Throwball and Soccer

We're growing! Sporting2Impact is working to add three new programs to our lineup:

  • Mixed Fitness: varied workouts that combine strength, cardio and mobility.
  • Throwball: a fast-paced team net game, similar to volleyball, where the ball is caught and thrown over the net.
  • Soccer: friendly, inclusive games for building skills and teamwork.

As with all our programs, the goal is to make sports and fitness accessible to everyone across Maryland. Interested in joining, coaching or volunteering? Email us and we'll keep you posted as sessions are scheduled.

A double day of movement: Zumba in Catonsville and a walk in Old Ellicott City

November 7 was a double day of movement. At noon, 10 participants danced through a free Zumba session at The Hub in Catonsville. That afternoon, 18 walkers joined our Wellness Walk on the Trolley Line #9 Trail in Old Ellicott City.

17 walkers kick off November at our Halloween Walk

On Sunday, November 1, 17 community members joined us on the Centennial Park Lake Trail in Ellicott City for our Halloween Walk. It was a fun way to start the month moving together.
